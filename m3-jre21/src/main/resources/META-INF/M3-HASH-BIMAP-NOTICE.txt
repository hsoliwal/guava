The native HashBiMap preserves the JRE class, public API, ordering, mutation
epochs and serialization protocol while using private payload arrays, integer
index/link lanes, generation lanes and explicit lifecycle flags. Existing M3
slot/residency mechanisms informed its private lifetime integration.

The retained M3-NOTICE.txt documents the earlier ArrayListMultimap increment.
This artifact contains that qualified increment and the additional HashBiMap
variant described here. Original Guava source remains the separate oracle.
The original Guava LICENSE, AUTHORS and individual notices are retained.
